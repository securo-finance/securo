## Objective

- **What:** Add category-owned budget fields, backfill them from legacy budget data, and simplify the budget service to one current budget state per category.
- **Why:** Phase 2 must migrate budget ownership safely before later phases cut readers and editors over to categories.
- **Output:** Schema migration, updated category/budget contracts, simplified budget service behavior, and migration-focused regression coverage.
