# Phase 9 Plan 01: Period-Linked Records and Cleanup

## Objective

- **What:** Add durable monthly-period linkage to records and remove the remaining supported category-group runtime/database dependency.
- **Why:** The monthly milestone needs a stable period anchor for transactions and accounts before month closure can exist, and the product still carries legacy category-group structures that conflict with the simplified monthly model.
- **Output:** A persisted monthly-period model wired into active financial records plus supported-surface cleanup that removes category-group concepts from active APIs and flows.
