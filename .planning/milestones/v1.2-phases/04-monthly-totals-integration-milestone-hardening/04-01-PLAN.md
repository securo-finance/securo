# Plan 04-01: Month Result Surface Hardening

## Objective

Finish the milestone by moving supported month-summary UX away from balance-centric reads and toward monthly result.

## Tasks

1. Update the dashboard hero to foreground month result derived from income and expenses.
2. Replace the balance-flow panel with a monthly trend view that stays aligned to the monthly model.
3. Remove the supported report entry point for net worth so analytics defaults to income-vs-expenses.

## Verification

- Dashboard highlights month result instead of total balance.
- Supported dashboard history view no longer relies on balance flow.
- Reports default to income-vs-expenses instead of net worth.
