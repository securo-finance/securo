# Phase 10 Plan 01: Month Closure and Next Month Start

## Objective

- **What:** Add preserved month snapshots plus a close-month workflow that immediately bootstraps the next editable `Mês Atual`.
- **Why:** The monthly model is incomplete until users can safely seal one month as history and move straight into the next operational month.
- **Output:** A backend snapshot/closure contract and dashboard UX that closes the current month, links the snapshot to the exact period, and opens the next month immediately.
